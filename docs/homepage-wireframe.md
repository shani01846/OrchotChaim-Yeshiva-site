# Homepage Wireframe: Yeshivat Orchot Chaim

**Document**: PHASE 1 Discovery — Conceptual Wireframe  
**Status**: For Review  
**Date**: September 1, 2026

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The homepage must follow a **narrative arc** that transforms a visitor from:

**"What is this place?"** → **"I want to be a partner."**

This requires intentional section sequencing to build trust, emotional connection, understanding, and motivation to support.

---

## SECTION 01: HERO — "THE FIRST 5 SECONDS"

Immediately communicate:
- **WHO**: Yeshivat Orchot Chaim
- **WHAT**: A place of Torah learning and personal growth
- **WHY**: Building the future of the next generation

**Intrigue + Aspiration**

## SECTION 15: FINAL EMOTIONAL CTA — THE LAST CONVERSION MOMENT

### Purpose
**Close the emotional narrative.**

Create a powerful final reason to donate before footer.

### Emotional Objective
**Hope + Partnership + Urgency (if real)**

Visitor should think: "I've been on a journey. Now I want to complete it by supporting this."

### Copywriting Approach

DO NOT simply repeat: "Donate now."

Instead, reinforce the emotional journey:

```
יש בניינים שנבנים מאבן.
[There are buildings built from stone.]

יש בניינים שנבנים משותפות.
[There are buildings built from partnership.]

עכשיו אתם יכולים להיות חלק מהבית
שבו יגדל הדור הבא.
[Now you can be part of the home
where the next generation will grow.]
```

### Visual Presentation
- Large, inspiring headline
- Supporting copy (2-3 sentences max)
- Two CTAs:
  - **Primary**: "אני רוצה להיות שותף" (I want to be a partner)
  - **Secondary**: "תרומה מחו"ל" (Donate from abroad)

### Design
- Full-width section
- Dark or accent color background
- Large typography
- Generous padding
- Centered, dramatic layout
